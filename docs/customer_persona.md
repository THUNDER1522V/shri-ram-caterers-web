# Customer Persona

## Primary Customer

Father of the Bride / Groom

Age: 45–65

Role:
Decision Maker

Goals:
- Organize a memorable wedding
- Impress guests
- Avoid mistakes
- Stay within budget
- Work with trustworthy vendors

Biggest Fears:
- Bad food
- Poor service
- Vendor not delivering on promises
- Guests complaining
- Hidden charges

Questions They Ask:
- Can I trust them?
- Have they handled weddings like mine?
- Is the food actually good?
- Are they professional?
- Are they worth the price?

What Makes Them Trust a Brand:
- Real event photos
- Real videos
- Genuine testimonials
- Years of experience
- Professional presentation
- Transparent communication

Expected Website Experience:
- Easy navigation
- Fast loading
- Mobile friendly
- Elegant visuals
- Clear contact options

Decision Trigger:
"I can confidently trust these people with one of the biggest events in my family's life."
