# Shri Ram Caterers Design Philosophy

## What are we building?

We are not building a catering website.

We are building confidence.

A father planning his daughter's wedding should feel:

"I don't need to look further."

---

## Emotional Journey

Hero
↓

Curiosity

↓

Trust

↓

Evidence

↓

Luxury

↓

Confidence

↓

Contact

---

## Design Language

Luxury
not flashy

Premium
not expensive-looking

Royal
not traditional

Modern
not startup

Editorial
not corporate

Warm
not playful

Minimal
not empty

---

## Inspiration

Dior

Apple

Aesop

Rolls Royce

YSL

(Not to copy them but to understand rhythm,
spacing,
typography,
confidence.)

---

## Anti Inspiration

Generic catering websites

Stock photography

Random gradients

Neon colors

Glassmorphism everywhere

Huge floating blobs

Template hero sections

AI-generated illustrations

---

## Every design decision must answer:

Does this increase trust?

Does this feel handcrafted?

Does this support luxury?

Would Dior approve this spacing?

Would Apple keep this animation?

Would this still look premium five years later?
